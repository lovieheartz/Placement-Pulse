Part one

The Problem

NSEC has built a strong placement record through dedicated TPOs and student PRs. The process behind that record is spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper forms.

TPOs Students Faculty Administration
About the project

Placement runs on manual, disconnected systems

The lack of a centralised digital system produces bottlenecks for everyone in the chain — and the losses compound silently.

  • Students submit weak resumes and miss deadlines they never saw
  • TPOs lose 15+ hours a week to coordination instead of employer outreach
  • NOC approvals take 5–7 days on paper, with zero tracking
  • Records live in Excel, where trend analysis is impossible

Why act now

Builds on NSEC's existing success — minimal disruption, integrates with current processes. Realtime voice AI has only just become cheap enough for a student-facing tool at cohort scale. And it positions NSEC as a tech-forward institution to recruiters.

Stakeholder 1 of 4

Problems faced by TPOs

1The broken telephone

TPOs brief PRs, who relay to their batches. Critical details get diluted or delayed at every hop.

Drive details went to 12 department PRs on WhatsApp — 3 forgot to forward. Entire batches missed the drive.

2No resume screening tool

500+ resumes per drive reviewed by hand with no standardised screening. Human error overlooks real talent.

A deserving student was overlooked for Amazon — her resume was misplaced in a pile.

3Data blindness

Records kept in Excel. No real-time reports, no trend analytics, no way to ask "why".

Nobody could say why only 30% of ME students placed despite good grades. The resume-skills gap surfaced a year later.

4Zero transparency

Students cannot track application or NOC status — so the same questions arrive again, and again, and again.

An application cannot be followed like a food-delivery order: Submitted → Under review → Shortlisted.
Stakeholder 2 of 4

Problems faced by Students — the ones who pay the price

1Rejections they can't explain

Formatting errors and missing keywords trigger automatic rejection. Nobody ever tells the student why.

A CSE student was rejected by all 8 companies he applied to — inconsistent fonts, no quantifiable achievements, not ATS-friendly.

2Missed drives

Notices arrive on WhatsApp and e-mail with no priority, no read-tracking, no reminders.

A student missed a recruitment drive because the message was buried under other group chats.

3Slow, opaque NOC process

Physical forms, days of waiting, no status — while the company's joining deadline runs out.

A student visited the TPO office 5 times for one Microsoft internship NOC. The paperwork was lost twice.

4No way to rehearse

Volunteer mock interviews cannot scale to 500 students, nor repeat on demand — so the first real interview is the placement interview.

Only 21% of surveyed final-year students knew an ATS would screen them at all (n = 14).

The gap, plotted

Two axes, because the market genuinely splits along them: tools that run the institution's placement operations, and tools that prepare the individual student. Everything on the market sits on one axis or the other.

The novelty argument, in one line

Recruiter platforms manage the drive. Prep tools charge each student and know nothing about the college. No existing system gives one institution both.

The synthesis

Operations and AI preparation, in one role-aware system, on infrastructure the institution owns.

The inversion

The college owns the tool — instead of recruiters owning it, or each student renting it monthly.

Engineering

Three-tier architecture, server-side AI

Presentation

React 19 + Vite. Four role-scoped portals share one design system. HTTPS plus exactly one WebSocket.

Application

Express 5 — REST, JWT auth, role guards, and a relay to the Gemini Live socket. The server mediates every model call.

Data

Supabase PostgreSQL via Prisma; object storage for uploads — files never touch local disk.

Security posture

No model runs in the browser. No API key ever reaches it. The browser holds only a JWT.

Engineering

16 entities — and a mid-project migration

1Why we left MongoDB

Deleting a test orphaned its questions and attempts, corrupting analytics. The store refused to enforce invariants the domain genuinely has. The domain is relational.

2What the engine now guarantees

UNIQUE (testId, studentId) makes a duplicate attempt impossible — not merely guarded against in application code. Soft deletion preserves every student's evidentiary record.

3Honest qualification

Cross-model references are still plain strings — no declared foreign keys yet. Declaring true FKs is named immediate future work, not hidden.

Engineering

Shipped to production — not a localhost demo

Frontend — Vercel

Static build on a global CDN. The API origin is injected at build time; not one URL is hard-coded.

Backend — Render

Express + WebSocket + cron. Every secret lives here as an environment variable — none are committed to git.

Free-tier honesty

Render sleeps when idle; Supabase pauses after a week. A scheduled ping keeps both warm — a real constraint, engineered around.

Part three

The AI Layer

Four capabilities, one provider, one key. Resume analysis · realtime voice interview · vision-graded handwriting · AI-generated aptitude tests.

The AI layer

One key, three modalities, a fallback chain

Schema-constrained, not prompt-and-pray

Grading and analysis calls declare a strict response schema. A grade can never come back with a missing score or a half-written breakdown.

The right model for the job

Flash reads text fast. Reading a handwritten script or a grade card is a different problem — those route to Pro, because Flash drops table rows.

Resilience by list

Each tier holds an ordered model list. Google retiring a preview model degrades the tier; it does not break the page.

AI module 1 of 4

AI Resume Analyzer — how it works

THE SIX-FACTOR WEIGHTED ATS SCORE

NFR-4 — never a bare number

A suggestion fires because a measured component fell below its cutoff. Every piece of advice is traceable to the evidence that produced it.

Clamped to [20, 100]

An unrelated resume never reports a demoralising zero — a deliberate pedagogical choice, not a bug.

AI module 2 of 4 · the flagship

AI Mock Interview — a real conversation

Spoken · adaptive · resume-conditioned · competency-decomposed — the four properties prior systems never satisfied together.

RELAY, NOT PASSTHROUGH

Natural turn-taking

Server-side voice activity detection decides when a turn ends — a conversation, not a walkie-talkie.

During the interview: zero praise

The persona may never say "good answer". An interview that flatters teaches nothing — evaluation happens on the record, afterwards.

Resume conditioning

Parsed projects and skills are injected into the persona. No question bank — every follow-up is generated live.

AI module 3 of 4 · verified on the live system

Handwritten scripts, graded by vision

A student scans a handwritten answer sheet. One vision call performs the OCR and the grading together — there is no brittle text-extraction step to fail first.

Live evidence — not a claim

A handwritten answer sheet was submitted through the deployed system. Gemini transcribed the handwriting verbatim and returned a per-question breakdown with comments.

20 / 20 awarded 4 questions OCR matched the script

The teacher stays in the loop

Staff can override any AI score. The extracted text is shown, so a student can verify what the model believed they wrote — and contest it.

Why one call, not two

OCR-then-grade loses the layout: struck-through work, marginal notes, a diagram. Grading from the image preserves the evidence a human marker would use.

AI module 4 of 4

Aptitude testing & analytics

PER-QUESTION DIFFICULTY — MEASURED ON THE LIVE SYSTEM

Three ways to author a test

By hand, by AI generation, or by extraction from a question-paper PDF. Timed attempts, shuffling, negative marking, auto-grading.

Proctoring flags — it does not accuse

Tab switches and fullscreen exits are recorded as telemetry for human review. The system never punishes a student on a signal it cannot interpret.

What staff see

Score distribution, pass rate, a named leaderboard, per-question success rates — plus a four-sheet Excel export.

STATE FLOWS — EVERY TRANSITION STAMPED WITH ACTOR AND TIME
NOC applies→sent→ read→reply soon→completed e-mail on every change · full audit trail
TEST draft→published→ ongoing→completed→archived deletion is soft — attempts survive
Role-based workflows

What each role actually does

One login, four scopes. The role is enforced on the server by middleware — never merely hidden in the UI.

Evaluation

Four methods, one live deployment

1Functional verification

All 14 modules driven end-to-end with real credentials — zero uncaught client exceptions.

2Behavioural inspection

AI outputs examined against their inputs: is the cited evidence actually consistent with the score?

3Usability study

14 volunteer final-year students used both AI modules and were surveyed.

4Systematic code audit

The audit — not the testing — found the defects now named as immediate hardening work.

LATENCY, MEASURED
The interview is the only feature where latency is felt as rudeness. Four seconds is fine for a resume report and fatal in a conversation — so only the interview streams.

Rehearsal cures rehearsal deficits

Students reported their real problem is not absent knowledge but articulating knowledge under pressure, aloud, to a stranger — a register they had never once practised.

The honest caveat

n = 14, self-selected, known to the author. Descriptive only — no statistical inference is drawn. One complaint (an empty Score Breakdown panel) led straight to a real audit finding.

The last figure is the one that matters. Four in five students did not know the filter existed — they were being rejected by a machine they had never heard of.
Impact

Systematic benefits

For students

24/7 resume feedback and interview rehearsal behind one login. Live NOC tracking — no more office visits for a status. Targeted notices, no missed drives.

For TPOs

Screening, grading and notification hours automated away. Analytics finally answer "where do students fail?" Every request tracked and auditable.

For the college

Owns its platform and its data. No per-student licences — the marginal cost is API usage. A model other MAKAUT colleges can adopt.

Competitive edge

Attracts better recruiters with a streamlined, transparent process — and positions NSEC as a tech-forward institution.

Road ahead — ordered by benefit over cost

1 · close the three audit-found security defects 2 · port the six-factor scorer into Node 3 · declare true foreign keys in Prisma 4 · move interview state to Redis 5 · validate ATS scores against real hiring outcomes
Technology should enhance human effort, not replace it. This is not just software — it is a strategic upgrade that makes placement transparent, efficient and student-centric.

What is novel

What is proven
