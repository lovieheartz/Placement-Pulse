Placement Management System

A placement platform where the AI is load-bearing — giving every student a resume expert and an interviewer on demand, at the marginal cost of an API call.

The Problem · Current Scenario

Placement runs on manual, disconnected systems

NSEC has built a strong placement record through dedicated TPOs and student PRs. The process behind that record is spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper forms — and the losses compound silently.

Why act now

Builds on NSEC's existing success — minimal disruption, integrating with current processes. Realtime voice AI has only just become cheap enough for a student-facing tool at cohort scale.

  • Students submit weak resumes and miss deadlines they never saw
  • NOC approvals take 5–7 days on paper, with zero tracking

Engineering · Architecture

Three-tier architecture, server-side AI

Presentation

React 19 + Vite. Four role-scoped portals share one design system. HTTPS plus exactly one WebSocket.

Application

Express 5 — REST, JWT auth, role guards, and a relay to the Gemini Live socket. The server mediates every model call.

Data

Supabase PostgreSQL via Prisma; object storage for uploads — files never touch local disk.

Security posture

No model runs in the browser. No API key ever reaches it. The browser holds only a JWT.

Why we left MongoDB

Deleting a test orphaned its questions and attempts, corrupting analytics. The store refused to enforce invariants the domain genuinely has. The domain is relational.

What the engine now guarantees

UNIQUE (testId, studentId) makes a duplicate attempt impossible — not merely guarded in application code. Soft deletion preserves every student's record.

Honest qualification

Cross-model references are still plain strings — no declared foreign keys yet. Named immediate future work, not hidden.

Engineering · Deployment

Shipped to production — not a localhost demo

Frontend — Vercel

Static build on a global CDN. The API origin is injected at build time; not one URL is hard-coded.

Backend — Render

Express + WebSocket + cron. Every secret lives here as an environment variable — none are committed to git.

Free-tier honesty

Render sleeps when idle; Supabase pauses after a week. A scheduled ping keeps both warm — a real constraint, engineered around.

The AI Layer · Model Routing

One key, three modalities, a fallback chain

Every AI feature is served by Google Gemini through a single provider module. Each capability routes to the model tier it actually needs — and each tier degrades down an ordered list rather than failing.

Schema-constrained, not prompt-and-pray

Grading and analysis calls declare a strict response schema. A grade can never come back with a missing score or a half-written breakdown.

The right model for the job

Flash reads text fast. Reading a handwritten script or a grade card is a different problem — those route to Pro, because Flash drops table rows.

Resilience by list

Each tier holds an ordered model list. Google retiring a preview model degrades the tier; it does not break the page.

AI Module 2 of 4 · The Flagship

AI Mock Interview — a real conversation

Spoken · adaptive · resume-conditioned · competency-decomposed — the four properties prior systems never satisfied together.

Natural turn-taking

AI Module 3 of 4 · Verified Live

Handwritten scripts, graded by vision

A student scans a handwritten answer sheet. One vision call performs the OCR and the grading together — there is no brittle text-extraction step to fail first.

Live evidence — not a claim

A handwritten answer sheet was submitted through the deployed system. Gemini transcribed the handwriting verbatim and returned a per-question breakdown with comments.

20 / 20 awarded 4 questions OCR matched the script

The teacher stays in the loop

Staff can override any AI score. The extracted text is shown, so a student can verify what the model believed they wrote — and contest it.

Why one call, not two

OCR-then-grade loses the layout: struck-through work, marginal notes, a diagram. Grading from the image preserves the evidence a human marker would use.

AI Module 4 of 4 & Role Workflows

Aptitude testing, analytics & who does what

PER-QUESTION DIFFICULTY — MEASURED ON THE LIVE SYSTEM
NOC applies→sent→ read→reply soon→completed
TEST draft→published→ ongoing→completed→archived

Three ways to author a test

By hand, by AI generation, or by extraction from a question-paper PDF. Timed attempts, shuffling, negative marking, auto-grading.

Proctoring flags — it does not accuse

Tab switches and fullscreen exits are recorded as telemetry for human review. The system never punishes a student on a signal it cannot interpret.

What staff see

Score distribution, pass rate, a named leaderboard, per-question success rates — plus a four-sheet Excel export.

Conclusion

What changes — and what comes next

For students

24/7 resume feedback and interview rehearsal behind one login. Live NOC tracking. Targeted notices — no missed drives.

For TPOs

Screening, grading and notification hours automated away. Analytics finally answer "where do students fail?"

For the college

Owns its platform and its data. No per-student licences — the cost is API usage. A model other MAKAUT colleges can adopt.

Competitive edge

Attracts better recruiters with a transparent process — and positions NSEC as a tech-forward institution.

What is novel

The synthesis — operations and AI preparation in one role-aware system. And the inversion — the institution owns the tool, rather than recruiters owning it or students renting it monthly.

What is proven

Deployed and publicly reachable. Handwriting graded 20/20 with verbatim OCR. Per-question difficulty measured. Interview turn 0.9 s — inside the conversational threshold.

What is not claimed

n = 14 is descriptive, not inferential. No foreign keys yet. Three security defects found by audit and named, not buried. ATS scores are not yet validated against hiring outcomes.

Road ahead — ordered by benefit over cost

1 · close the three audit-found security defects 2 · port the six-factor scorer into Node 3 · declare true foreign keys in Prisma 4 · move interview state to Redis 5 · validate ATS scores against real hiring outcomes
Technology should enhance human effort, not replace it. This is not just software — it is a strategic upgrade that makes placement transparent, efficient and student-centric.
