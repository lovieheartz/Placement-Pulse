02Problem
The current scenario

Placement runs on manual, disconnected systems

Dedicated TPOs and student PRs have built a strong placement record — but the process behind it is spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper forms.

The broken telephone
  • TPOs lose 15+ hours a week to coordination instead of employer outreach
  • NOC approvals take 5–7 days on paper, with zero tracking
  • Placement records live in Excel, where trend analysis is impossible

Why act now

Roughly 68% of resumes are auto-rejected by ATS software before a human ever reads them.

Realtime voice AI has only just become affordable enough for a student-facing tool at cohort scale.

The scarce resource is expert attention — and expert attention does not scale. This single sentence is the premise of the entire project.
03Problem
Stakeholder pain

Three roles, one broken process

Every pain point below became a numbered functional requirement — FR-1 … FR-12, NFR-1 … NFR-6.

TPOs

No screening tool

500+ resumes per drive reviewed by hand, with no standardised screening.

A deserving student was overlooked for Amazon — her resume was misplaced in a pile.

Data blindness

Excel records; no real-time reports, no trend analytics.

Nobody could say why only 30% of ME students placed. The skills gap surfaced a year late.
Students

Rejections they can't explain

Formatting errors and missing keywords trigger auto-rejection. Nobody tells them why.

A CSE student was rejected by all 8 companies he applied to — no quantifiable achievements, not ATS-friendly.

No way to rehearse

Volunteer mocks can't scale to 500 students or repeat on demand — so the first real interview is the placement interview.

Faculty

Feedback bottleneck

Personalised feedback on every submission is time-infeasible; late grades break the learning cycle the test was meant to serve.

Untracked requests

Queries scatter across WhatsApp and e-mail with no ownership.

15+ students e-mailed the same NOC question daily for a week — and got no reply.
Only 21% of surveyed final-year students knew an ATS would screen their resume at all (n = 14).

06Architecture
System design

Three-tier architecture, server-side AI

Presentation

React 19 + Vite SPA. Four role-scoped portals share one design system. HTTPS plus exactly one WebSocket.

Application

Express 5 — REST, JWT auth, role guards, and a relay to the Gemini Live socket. The server mediates every model call.

Data

Supabase PostgreSQL via Prisma ORM; object storage for uploads — files never touch local disk.

Security posture

No model runs in the browser. No API key ever reaches it. The browser holds only a JWT.

07Architecture
Deployment — live, not a localhost demo

Shipped to production

The system is deployed and publicly reachable. Every URL below is live; the frontend is built from the same commit as the backend.

Frontend — Vercel

Static React build, global CDN. The API origin is injected at build time; not a single URL is hard-coded.

Backend — Render

Express + WebSocket + cron. Every secret lives here as an environment variable — none are committed to git.

Free-tier honesty

Render sleeps when idle and Supabase pauses after a week. A scheduled ping keeps both warm — a real constraint, engineered around.

08Data
The database

16 entities — and a mid-project migration

1Why we left MongoDB

Deleting a test orphaned its questions and attempts, corrupting analytics. The store refused to enforce invariants the domain genuinely has. The domain is relational.

2What the engine now guarantees

UNIQUE (testId, studentId) makes a duplicate attempt impossible, not merely guarded against. Soft deletion preserves every student's evidentiary record.

3Honest qualification

Cross-model references are still plain strings — no declared foreign keys. Declaring true FKs in Prisma is named immediate future work, not hidden.

09AI
The AI layer

One key, three modalities, a fallback chain

Every AI feature is served by Google Gemini through a single provider module. Each capability routes to the model tier it actually needs — and each tier degrades down a list rather than failing.

Schema-constrained, not prompt-and-pray

Grading and analysis calls declare a strict response schema. A grade can never come back with a missing score or a half-written breakdown.

Right model for the job

Flash reads text fast. Reading a handwritten script or a grade card is a different problem — those route to a pro model, because Flash drops table rows.

Resilience by list

Each tier holds an ordered model list. Google retiring a preview model degrades the tier; it does not break the page.

10AI
Module 1

AI Resume Analyzer — how it works

The six-factor weighted score

NFR-4 — never a bare number

A suggestion fires because a measured component fell below its cutoff. Every piece of advice is traceable to the evidence that produced it.

Clamped to [20, 100]

An unrelated resume never reports a demoralising zero — a deliberate pedagogical choice, not a bug.

11AI
Module 2 — the flagship

AI Mock Interview — a real conversation

Spoken · adaptive · resume-conditioned · competency-decomposed — the four properties prior systems never satisfied together.

Relay, not passthrough

Natural turn-taking

Server-side voice activity detection decides when a turn ends — a conversation, not a walkie-talkie.

During the interview: zero praise

The persona may never say "good answer". An interview that flatters teaches nothing — evaluation happens on the record, afterwards.

Resume conditioning

Parsed projects and skills are injected into the persona. No question bank — every follow-up is generated live.

12AI
Module 3 — verified end-to-end on the live system

Handwritten scripts, graded by vision

A student photographs or scans a handwritten answer sheet. One vision call performs the OCR and the grading together — there is no brittle text-extraction step to fail first.

Live evidence — not a claim

A handwritten answer sheet was submitted through the deployed system. Gemini transcribed the handwriting verbatim and returned a per-question breakdown.

20 / 20 awarded 4 questions OCR matched the script

The teacher stays in the loop

Staff can override any AI score. The extracted text is kept and shown, so a student can verify what the model believed they wrote — and contest it.

Why one call, not two

OCR-then-grade loses the layout: struck-through work, marginal notes, a diagram. Grading from the image preserves the evidence a human marker would use.

13Modules
Aptitude testing & analytics

Where the cohort actually fails

Per-question difficulty — a real test on the live system

Three ways to author a test

By hand, by AI generation, or by extraction from a question-paper PDF. Timed attempts, shuffling, negative marking, auto-grading.

Proctoring flags — it does not accuse

Tab switches and fullscreen exits are recorded as telemetry for human review. The system never auto-punishes a student on a signal it cannot interpret.

What staff see

Score distribution, pass rate, a named leaderboard, and per-question success rates — plus a four-sheet Excel export.

State flows — every transition stamped with actor and time
NOC applies→ sent→ read→ reply soon→ completed e-mail at every change · full audit trail
Test draft→ published→ ongoing→ completed→ archived deletion is soft — attempts survive

2Behavioural inspection

AI outputs examined against their inputs: is the cited evidence actually consistent with the score?

17Impact
Impact & the road ahead

What changes, and what's next

For students

24/7 resume feedback and interview rehearsal behind one login. Live NOC tracking — no more office visits for a status. Targeted notices, no missed drives.

For TPOs & faculty

Screening, grading and notification hours automated away. Analytics finally answer "where do students fail?" Every request tracked and auditable.

For the institution

The college owns its platform and its data. No per-student licences — the marginal cost is API usage. A model other MAKAUT colleges can adopt.

Road ahead — ordered by benefit over cost

1 · close the three audit-found security defects 2 · port the six-factor scorer into Node 3 · declare true foreign keys in Prisma 4 · move interview state to Redis 5 · validate ATS scores against real hiring outcomes
Technology should enhance human effort, not replace it. This is not just software — it is a strategic upgrade that makes placement transparent, efficient and student-centric.
